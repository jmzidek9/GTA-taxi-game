# Crosstown — Vale Harbor Fares

A 3D taxi game that runs in the browser. Take a fare from dispatch, stop by your passenger, watch them climb in, then beat the meter to their destination.

**Play:** https://jmzidek9.github.io/GTA-taxi-game/

## Controls
- **Keyboard:** W/S gas and brake, A/D steer, Space drift, E let passenger in/out, F fares, C camera, R reset car, Esc pause
- **Phone:** drag the steering pad, hold Gas/Brake, tap the yellow prompt to board
- **Gamepad:** stick steers, RT gas, LT brake, A interact, Y camera

## Features
Seven districts with traffic lights, AI traffic and pedestrians · day/night cycle and weather · chase and cockpit cameras · GPS route on the road and minimap · eight fare types · itemised payout receipt · garage with 5 cars, upgrades and paint · progress saved in the browser.

Built with Three.js; no build step.
